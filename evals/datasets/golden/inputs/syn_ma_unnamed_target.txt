Corvane Systems to Acquire Industrial Sensor Maker

CLEVELAND, Ohio, July 9, 2026 -- Corvane Systems, Inc. (NASDAQ: CRVN) today announced that it has signed a definitive agreement to acquire a privately held manufacturer of industrial pressure sensors for $45 million in cash. The name of the company being acquired was not disclosed at the request of its owners. The acquisition is expected to close within 60 days.
