Item 5.02 Departure of Directors or Certain Officers; Election of Directors; Appointment of Certain Officers.

On September 8, 2026, Daniel Ortiz informed the Board of Directors of Pinecrest Medical Devices, Inc. (the "Company") of his decision to resign as Chief Financial Officer, effective September 30, 2026, to pursue other opportunities. Mr. Ortiz's resignation is not the result of any disagreement with the Company.

On the same date, the Board appointed Laura Chen, the Company's Vice President of Finance, as Chief Financial Officer, effective October 1, 2026. Ms. Chen will receive an annual base salary of $410,000.
