Kestrel Foods Announces Agreement to Acquire Summit Dairy and Declares Special Cash Dividend

DENVER, Colorado, August 18, 2026 -- Kestrel Foods, Inc. (NYSE: KSTF) today announced two actions approved by its Board of Directors.

First, Kestrel has entered into a definitive merger agreement to acquire Summit Dairy Co. for $1.2 billion in cash. The transaction is expected to close in the fourth quarter of 2026, subject to regulatory approval.

Second, the Board declared a special cash dividend of $1.50 per share of common stock, payable on September 15, 2026 to shareholders of record at the close of business on September 1, 2026.
