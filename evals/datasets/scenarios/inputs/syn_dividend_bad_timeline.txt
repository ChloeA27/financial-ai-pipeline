Larkspur Utilities Declares Quarterly Dividend

On May 12, 2026, the Board of Directors of Larkspur Utilities Corp. (NYSE: LKSP) declared a quarterly cash dividend of $0.15 per share of common stock. The ex-dividend date is April 29, 2026. The dividend is payable on June 5, 2026 to shareholders of record as of April 30, 2026.
