Harborline Bancorp Declares Quarterly Cash Dividend

PORTLAND, Maine, October 21, 2026 -- Harborline Bancorp, Inc. (NASDAQ: HRBL) announced that on October 21, 2026 its Board of Directors declared a regular quarterly cash dividend of $0.24 per share of common stock. The dividend is payable on November 14, 2026 to shareholders of record at the close of business on October 31, 2026.
