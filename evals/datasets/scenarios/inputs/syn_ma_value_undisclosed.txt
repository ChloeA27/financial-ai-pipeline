Northwind Analytics Announces Agreement to Acquire BrightPath Logistics

AUSTIN, Texas, October 5, 2026 -- Northwind Analytics, Inc. (NASDAQ: NWDA) today announced that it has entered into a definitive agreement to acquire 100% of BrightPath Logistics LLC, a privately held provider of freight routing software. Northwind will pay the purchase price entirely in cash from its existing balance sheet.

Financial terms of the transaction were not disclosed. The transaction does not require a vote of Northwind shareholders and is expected to close in the first quarter of 2027, subject to customary closing conditions.
